PanIC-CF confirmatory method lock
=================================

Lock status
-----------

The active method set, literal exact-nonzero rule, primary estimands, margins,
decision rules, failure policy, and new seed families were fixed before the
fresh-seed regeneration. This lock does not claim to precede earlier
exploratory or historical outputs, which are not part of the regenerated
confirmatory evidence.

The numerical lineage is retained under `reference_implementation/`. Those
frozen files provide shared generic primitives and historical auditability;
they are not an additional assessed implementation and do not override the
current top-level configuration or method definitions.

Assessed methods
----------------

Every main and grid replication has exactly three assessed methods:

1. `PanIC-CF`, using five independently seeded balanced half-splits, both
   directions per split, the cross-signed target, projection to `[0,20]`, the
   31-point multiplier grid, and the largest-multiplier one-standard-error
   stabilization rule;
2. `CV`, ordinary balanced five-fold cross-validation selecting the smallest
   radius that attains the minimum mean validation loss; and
3. `BIC-like`, the family-appropriate active-count criterion on the common
   full-sample path.

The PanIC-CF undershoot weight is

    omega(n_V) = sqrt(log(log(n_V + exp(exp(1))))).

The scenarios, signal construction, independent test size 2,000, radius
interval `[0,20]`, 121-point primary radius grid, multiplier grid, solver
controls, and calibration default `kappa=1` are otherwise unchanged.

Exact support rule
------------------

Support is defined by the literal fitted coefficient values:

    selected_j = (beta_hat_j != 0).

False positives, false negatives, exact recovery, and total support error all
use this rule. BIC-like active counts use the same exact nonzero test before
the cumulative maximum is formed. No post hoc numerical coefficient threshold
is authorized. The interpolation-radius tolerance remains a solver-accuracy
check only.

Confirmatory design and decision
--------------------------------

The main master seed is 2136092001, with 1,000 replications in each of seven
scenarios. The primary support effect is the equal-weight mean of

    (FP + FN)_PanIC-CF - (FP + FN)_CV.

Support superiority passes when its one-sided 95% upper normal Monte Carlo
bound is below zero. The prediction endpoint is the equal-weight mean of

    (D_PanIC-CF - D_CV) / D_CV.

Prediction noninferiority passes when its one-sided 95% upper normal Monte
Carlo bound is below 0.001. Margins 0.0005, 0.0025, and 0.005 are sensitivity
summaries only. A joint conclusion requires complete pairing in all seven
scenarios and both component decisions to pass. Scenario-specific, BIC-like,
and grid comparisons cannot change the joint decision.

Every CV denominator in the relative-deviance endpoint must be finite and
strictly positive. A violation is a hard failure: no replication is removed
and no denominator is floored or replaced.

Seed families
-------------

The grid-sensitivity master seed is 2138092001, with 500 common-random-number
replications at 61, 121, and 241 radius points. Dedicated smoke mode uses main
and grid seeds 2140092001 and 2142092001.

The non-overlap audit includes the following prior or retired families:

    2026091801  2026091802  2036091802  2046091802  2056091802
    2066091802  2076091802  2086091802  2106091802  2126091802

It enumerates training, five calibration-split, CV-fold, and test streams;
checks pairwise separation of the four new production/smoke families; checks
separation from conservative supersets of every prior family; and verifies
that all seeds remain in R's positive integer range. Smoke mode cannot write
to the committed results directory or consume a production seed.

Failure and output contract
---------------------------

A full-path failure marks all three method rows failed. A calibration-only
failure uses the prespecified multiplier default and records both flags. A CV
fold failure marks the CV row failed. No failed estimate is silently replaced.

The main raw output has three method rows per replication. The compact release
retains the PanIC-CF/CV endpoint rows, the denominator audit, all three pairwise
method comparisons, calibration diagnostics, and three-method grid summaries.
The primary performance table reports selected radius and signed
attained-radius error without repeating attained radius; the calibration table
leaves sparse operational counts to diagnostics and prose; and the grid table
omits runtime. These are presentation choices, not changes to estimands.
