PanIC-CF confirmatory method lock
=================================

Lock status
-----------

The statistical method, estimands, margins, and decision rules were fixed
before any simulation outcome was inspected.  A one-time seed-only re-lock,
documented below, reserves previously untouched streams for the final
1,000-replication main and 500-replication grid studies. The frozen numerical
engine from the preceding release is vendored under
`reference_implementation/` so that a fresh checkout is self-contained.

Authorized method amendment
---------------------------

The primary PanIC-CF procedure differs from the preceding release only in the
asymmetric undershoot weight:

    omega(n_V) = sqrt(log(log(n_V + exp(exp(1))))).

Five independently seeded balanced half-splits, two directions per split, the
cross-signed target, projection interval, radius grid, multiplier grid,
largest-multiplier one-standard-error rule, failure default, solver controls,
and full-data selection rule are unchanged.

`PanIC-CF-original` evaluates the preceding log-log weight on the identical
candidate paths, pilots, targets, and test sample.  It is a labelled
sensitivity analysis and adds no fitting.  `CV-min` is the preceding ordinary
minimum-validation-loss five-fold comparator.  `CV-1SE` is secondary: from
the same five fold-loss curves it selects the smallest radius whose mean loss
is at most the CV-min mean plus the fold-level standard error at CV-min,
where SE is the standard deviation of the five fold means divided by sqrt(5).

Confirmatory design and decision
--------------------------------

The final main master seed is 2066091802 and there are 1,000 replications in each of
the seven unchanged scenarios.  The primary support effect is the equal-weight
mean across those scenarios of the paired difference

    (FP + FN)_PanIC-CF - (FP + FN)_CV-min.

Support superiority passes when its one-sided 95% upper normal Monte Carlo
bound is below zero.  The prediction guardrail is the equal-weight mean of

    (D_PanIC-CF - D_CV-min) / D_CV-min.

Prediction noninferiority passes when its one-sided 95% upper normal Monte
Carlo bound is below the fixed margin 0.001 (0.1%).  A joint claim requires
complete pairing and both decisions to pass.  Margins 0.0005, 0.0025, and
0.005 are sensitivity analyses only and cannot change the primary decision.
Scenario-specific results, PanIC-CF-original, and CV-1SE are secondary.

The final v2 grid-sensitivity master seed is 2086091802, with 500 common-
random-number replications at 61, 121, and 241 radius points.  Dedicated smoke
mode uses main and grid seeds 2106091802 and 2126091802.  Exact enumeration of
all training, five calibration-split, CV-fold, and test seeds verifies that
all four final production/smoke families are pairwise disjoint and disjoint
from conservative 1,000-replication, seven-scenario supersets for prior or
retired master seeds 2026091801, 2026091802, 2036091802, 2046091802,
2056091802, and 2076091802.  It also verifies that every seed remains in R's
positive integer range.  These checks are part of
`Implementation_Validation.R` and must pass before either runner starts.

Pre-run validation amendment
----------------------------

The first deterministic audit found that three exact-equivalence checks
compared inherited data-frame row names in addition to the scientific fields.
The check was corrected to normalize row names before exact field comparison.
All displayed values and every substantive field already agreed; no method,
seed, estimand, decision rule, or simulation setting was changed.  This
amendment occurred before any production simulation was started.

The all-setting smoke pipeline then exposed a packaging-only issue: its table
caption and pairing-completeness flag used the locked production count rather
than the run-specific count recorded in `configuration.csv`.  Output analysis
was changed to read that recorded count.  This changes no production value
(both counts are 1,000 in the locked run), and no method or decision rule.
The independent verifier received the corresponding run-count correction so
that non-production smoke archives are assessed against their own recorded
replication count; the locked production check remains 1,000.

That smoke run had consumed replications 1--2 of the then-candidate main seed
2056091802, and the grid smoke used 2076091802.  No statistical setting was
changed in response.  Nevertheless, to make the final seeds literally
uninspected, a one-time seed-only re-lock retired both seeds, added them to the
exclusion audit, and fixed 2066091802 and 2086091802 for production.  The
runners now reject every reduced-replication use of a production seed and
substitute 2106091802 and 2126091802 only when `--smoke` is explicit.  The
partial old candidate output directory contained only configuration, registry,
and lock files; no scenario completed there. The full audit trail is described
in `PROVENANCE.md`.

The manuscript-facing output layer maps the internal method identifier
`BIC-active (exploratory)` to the reader-facing label `BIC-like`.  Its primary
table caption states that the logistic and Poisson rows are exploratory
active-count analogues outside the Gaussian proposition.  Internal process
phrases such as “second-confirmation” and “revised PanIC-CF” were removed from
reader-facing captions.  Internal identifiers and statistical calculations
are unchanged, and the independent verifiers enforce these presentation
requirements.

Post-production presentation and verification amendment
-------------------------------------------------------

The 2026-09-20 release makes the manuscript self-contained with respect to
tables.  Seven complete table floats are embedded in `manuscript/main.tex`;
the files under `results/table_*.tex` are deterministic, checksum-controlled
mirrors rather than LaTeX inputs.  A common renderer now generates all seven
mirrors, and independent checks compare both the mirrors and the embedded
blocks byte-for-byte.  This refactoring changes no fitted path, random stream,
summary value, estimand, threshold, or decision.

The same release makes an implicit domain requirement for the relative
test-deviance contrast explicit: every paired CV-min denominator must be
finite and strictly positive.  A violation is a hard failure; no observation
is deleted and no denominator is floored or replaced.  The retained 7,000
paired production rows satisfy this condition.  Their scenario-level ranges
and empirical contrast moments are recorded in
`relative_deviance_denominator_audit.csv` and independently reconstructed from
the raw rows.  This is a verification amendment to the already fixed estimand,
not a change to the statistical procedure or reported numerical results.
